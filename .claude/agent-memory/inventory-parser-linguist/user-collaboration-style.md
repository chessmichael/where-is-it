Andy is the primary (solo) engineer on Where Is It. He wants a substantive back-and-forth design dialogue, not a rubber stamp — explicitly asks for disagreement with current choices where warranted, concrete example utterances, and punch lists ordered by value-to-effort. Philosophy: push the deterministic offline parser as far as it reasonably goes before deferring anything to the optional Claude layer; "efficient and extensible" matters more than raw coverage. His own stated top data-model concerns: (a) duplicates/similar names under the exact-name-keyed store, (b) anaphora beyond the single last-item pointer ("the one in the bedroom"). He ends sessions wanting 2-3 crisp decision points to weigh in on. See [[parser-state-and-gaps]].
